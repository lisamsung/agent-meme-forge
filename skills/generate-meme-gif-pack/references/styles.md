# Styles

Styles affect visual prompts only. The output package remains WeChat-size GIF and PNG assets.

## `clean-sticker`

Default. Clean digital sticker with crisp outline, large head, readable expression, simple color blocks, minimal background. Best for WeChat review and broad chat use.

## `pixel-art`

Chunky pixel-art character, limited palette, crisp hard edges, no antialiasing feel, strong silhouette. Keep captions added by the processor, not by the image model. Use larger character shapes because 240px output can crush fine pixels.

## `chibi`

Cute exaggerated head and compact body. Good for students, research, and light workplace packs. Avoid making every reaction too cute; frustration and sarcasm still need readable facial acting.

## `retro-msn`

Early internet messenger energy, glossy small avatar charm, simple loops, playful colors. Useful when the user wants nostalgic chat vibes.

## `office-cartoon`

Modern workplace cartoon, laptop, papers, coffee, meeting props, clean vector-like shapes. Best for 打工仔, 都市丽人, 会议受害者, 甲方幸存者.

## `hand-drawn`

Loose marker or sketch feel, expressive lines, slightly imperfect. Good for “发疯但安全” energy. Keep the outline strong and avoid muddy shading.

## Style Consistency Checklist

- Same head shape and hair cues across all stickers.
- Same approximate outfit or color anchor unless the joke needs a prop.
- Same line weight and shading style.
- Same text treatment because all captions are added by the processor.
