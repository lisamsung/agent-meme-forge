## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-01

First public release.

### Added

- Codex skill for planning 16- or 24-item Chinese animated sticker packs from a
  reference image or text character concept.
- Sendability gate covering reuse trigger, emotional value, creative hook, and
  phone-size visual readability.
- Deterministic 4-keypose-to-16-frame renderer with local effects and Chinese
  caption layout.
- Dense real-frame mode with sheet slicing, scale normalization, head anchoring,
  timing, and continuity checks.
- QC gates for transparency, edge contact, bounding-box drift, prop flicker,
  face drift, motion energy, and loop closure.
- WeChat-ready export structure with GIFs, thumbnails, cover assets, manifests,
  and machine-readable QC reports.
- English and Chinese documentation, runnable examples, sample GIFs, and a
  GitHub Pages project site.
- Contribution, conduct, security, support, issue-triage, review, and release
  processes for ongoing open-source maintenance.
